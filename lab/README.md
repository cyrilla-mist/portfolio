[Open the Web Lab](https://cyrilla-mist.github.io/portfolio/lab/)

## Repository Role

The Web Lab is intentionally different from the standalone product repositories.

A tool belongs here when it is primarily a small self-contained browser utility or interface experiment rather than a product with its own architecture, release history, backend boundary, or long-term development track.

Larger projects such as Sideglance, STATEWAKE, Nexus Atlas, English Radar, and Verity remain in their own repositories.

## Legacy URLs
